About

I'm a software engineer and self-taught illustrator based in Seattle, WA. In 2020 I started an art shop called Lucy The Mews where I share my animal inspired art. My two cats, Lucy and Aegi, are my benevolent shop managers and muses. I publish a monthly newsletter with behind-the-scenes updates of my life, art process, and the occasional comic. When I’m not at my desk I enjoy running, munching on crunchy snacks, and watching tv.
